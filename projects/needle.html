Steerable Tape-Spring Needle

A new class of steerable needle that makes 15° to 150° turns through tissue with a turn radius as low as 3 mm.

LabMODLAB, University of Pennsylvania VenueICRA 2025 PaperbioRxiv

Problem

Steerable needles reach hard-to-reach tissue with minimal invasion, but wide turns damage the tissue around the path. A needle that can turn sharply keeps that damage small.

Approach

  • Designed a tape-spring needle and derived and experimentally validated a geometric model for it.
  • Designed and built the steering system for linear actuation and out-of-plane steering.
  • Developed the needle kinematics and integrated a camera for path planning (RRT) and feedback control.
  • Moved the system to ROS to improve communication between software and hardware.

Result

We steered the needle manually and robotically along Dubins paths in 7 kPa and 13 kPa tissue phantoms, which mimic healthy and unhealthy liver tissue, and measured how it handles stiffness transitions between tissues. We also showed needle tip tracking with ultrasound, navigation around anatomical obstacles, and integration with an autonomous robotic steering system.
