Control and Planning on the Franka Panda

Kinematics, velocity control and path planning with camera-based object detection for pick-and-place.

CourseMEAM 5200, University of Pennsylvania Year2023 StackPython, ROS

Problem

The final project of Penn's graduate robotics course: control a Franka Emika Panda to detect blocks with a camera and pick and place them.

Approach

  • Forward and inverse kinematics, velocity control and position control, in Python with ROS.
  • Path planning with bidirectional RRT and artificial potential fields, including secondary tasks on top of the main task.
  • Camera-based object detection connected to the kinematics and control code.

Result

The full pipeline detects blocks with the camera and picks and places them; the video shows it running in simulation.
