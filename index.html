Davin Tjandra

Robotics Software Engineer at Intrinsic

I'm a robotics engineer working on robot control, especially for tasks where the robot is in contact with its environment or working in a collaborative manner. My work combines classical control with learning: compliance and force control, stable dynamical systems, and safety filters that let learned policies run on robot arms.

I'm currently a Robotics Software Engineer at Intrinsic, where I develop and deploy robotic solutions for industrial clients. Before that, I worked on steerable needles for medical procedures, compliance control on the KUKA iiwa, and force-controlled robot arms for construction. I hold an M.S. in Mechanical Engineering (Mechatronics and Robotic Systems) from the University of Pennsylvania and a bachelor's in Mechanical Engineering (cum laude) from the University of Indonesia.
